How UNICORE Works & Is Organised

A transparent breakdown of the 5-stage scholar journey from application submission to academic graduation and career transition in Italy.

The Scholar Roadmap

The 5 Stages of the UNICORE Journey

Understand what happens at every step of the process from eligibility check to post-study career opportunities.

1
Stage 1 Application Phase

Online Application & Dossier Submission

Candidates choose up to two Italian universities and submit their academic credentials via the official portal. Applications are completely free of charge.

  • Valid UNHCR Refugee Identity Document
  • Bachelor's degree diploma & transcripts
  • GPA equivalent >= 3.0 / 4.0
  • English Motivation Letter & CV (Europass)
2
Stage 2 Evaluation Phase

Academic Evaluation & Online Interviews

University committees evaluate applications based on strict merit criteria: Document Screening (0–40 points) followed by a virtual Academic Interview (0–40 points).

  • Document scoring: GPA & curriculum fit
  • Shortlisted candidates invited to interview
  • English oral proficiency test
  • Final ranking list published on university site
3
Stage 3 Pre-Departure Phase

Visa Issuance & Flight Coordination

Selected candidates receive assistance from UNHCR and the Italian Embassy to obtain their National Study Visa (Type D) and travel tickets.

  • Consular fee exemptions supported by MAECI
  • Free flight tickets to Italy arranged
  • Pre-departure briefing & orientation
  • Safe arrival coordination at Italian airports
4
Stage 4 2-Year Study Period

Studies & Campus Life in Italy

Scholars begin their 2-year Master's degree with ongoing financial and social support from universities and partner organizations.

  • Monthly stipend (approx. €450–€600/month)
  • Free student housing & meal cards (Mensa)
  • Permesso di Soggiorno Studio administrative help
  • Free Italian language courses & student buddies
5
Stage 5 Post-Study Phase

Graduation & Career Pathways

Upon graduation, students can legally transition into the Italian or European workforce, or pursue Ph.D. research fellowships.

  • 12-month Job Search Residence Permit (Ricerca Lavoro)
  • Conversion to work permit upon employment contract
  • Access to doctoral programs across Europe
  • Active participation in UNICORE Alumni Network
Merit Principles

Selection Criteria & Anti-Fraud Rules

UNICORE operates on transparent academic merit, equal opportunity, and strict credential verification.

Scoring System Breakdown

  • Phase 1: Dossier Screening (Max 40 points)
    Evaluation of Bachelor's GPA, academic transcript relevance to chosen Master's program, CV, and motivation statement.
  • Phase 2: Academic Interview (Max 40 points)
    Virtual interview conducted by university faculty to assess field knowledge, motivation, and conversational English proficiency.
  • Minimum Passing Threshold:
    Typically requires at least 25/40 in Phase 1 and 25/40 in Phase 2 (50/80 total) to qualify for scholarship ranking.

Credential Verification & Anti-Fraud

UNICORE applications are strictly 100% free of charge. All candidate credentials (refugee identity and academic diplomas) undergo thorough multi-agency verification:

  • Refugee status verified directly with UNHCR country offices.
  • Bachelor's degree authenticated via CIMEA Statement of Comparability or EQPR.
  • Submitting false or forged documents results in immediate disqualification and legal reporting.
