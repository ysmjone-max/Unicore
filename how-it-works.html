Program & Academic Roadmap

How Does UNICORE Work?

A step-by-step roadmap from initial online selection to university arrival, Italian academic exam navigation, and post-graduation career launchpad.

Step-by-Step Guide

The 5-Stage Scholar Journey

Detailed timeline of how eligible refugee scholars are selected, relocated, and supported across Italian universities.

1
Stage 1: Application

Online Call & Document Submission

Candidates apply through the official UNICORE platform for up to two Italian universities, submitting certified academic transcripts, refugee status documentation, CV, and motivational statements.

  • Bachelor's Degree Certificate
  • UNHCR/National Refugee ID
  • English Language Proficiency (or EMI letter)
  • Academic Transcript of Records
2
Stage 2: Selection

Merit Evaluation & Online Faculty Interview

Selection procedures and scoring rubrics (e.g. 100-point vs 80-point scales) are determined independently by each participating university. Committees evaluate academic transcripts and conduct an online technical/motivational interview.

  • Academic Portfolio Screening (Curriculum & Transcripts)
  • Online Technical & English Proficiency Interview
  • Scoring Rubric Defined by Host University Call
  • Official Merit Ranking Published on University & UNHCR Portals
3
Stage 3: Relocation

Visa Issuance & Safe International Travel

Successful candidates receive comprehensive relocation support: MAECI study visa facilitation, UNHCR document coordination, pre-departure briefings, and flight sponsorship.

  • Italian National Study Visa (Type D)
  • Free International Flight Ticket
  • Medical Examination & Health Protocol
  • Pre-Departure Orientation & Buddy Introduction
4
Stage 4: Studies in Italy

Arrival, Enrollment & Campus Integration

Students arrive in Italy in September/October, attend the national welcome event, and transition to their host universities with housing, monthly stipends, and peer buddy support.

  • Permesso di Soggiorno Studio
  • Monthly Pocket Money (€450–€600)
  • Tessera Sanitaria (Health Coverage)
  • Italian Language & University Buddy Mentorship
5
Stage 5: Career

Graduation & Professional Career Pathways

Upon completing their Master's degree, graduates can convert their study permit into a 12-month job search residence permit (Permesso Ricerca Lavoro) or pursue Ph.D. positions.

  • Italian Master's Degree (Laurea Magistrale)
  • 12-Month Job Search Permit (Permesso Ricerca Lavoro)
  • Doctoral Research (Ph.D.) Pathways
  • UNICORE Alumni Association Network
Transparency & Expectations

What is Guaranteed vs. What Varies by University Call?

Because UNICORE is a consortium of 40+ independent Italian universities with regional welfare partners, some benefits are standardized nationally while others depend on your specific university's Call for Applications.

Support Dimension Program Status What to Expect & How it Works
Tuition Fee Exemption Standard (100% Free) 100% exemption from all university tuition fees and enrollment costs across all participating institutions.
Application Fee Standard (100% Free) The UNICORE application and selection process is completely free of charge. No fees are ever charged to apply.
Study Visa Facilitation Standard Protocol Coordinated via Italian Embassies, UNHCR, and MAECI with emergency consular travel support if conventional travel documents are delayed.
International Flight Ticket Standard Provision Direct economy flight ticket to Italy funded through the UNICORE humanitarian partner coalition.
Monthly Living Allowance Varies by University Amount varies by host university call and regional partner (typically between €400 and €600/month or regional DSU grant equivalent). Always check the individual university call.
Accommodation / Housing Varies by University Provided either as a free student dormitory room or as a dedicated monthly housing contribution depending on university dormitory capacity and local partner contracts.
Meals & Canteen (Mensa) Varies by University Some universities offer free canteen cards directly; others require calculating the ISEE Parificato to unlock Fascia 0 free/discounted meals.
Local Transit Pass Varies by City Subsidized or free annual city transit pass depending on the municipality and regional student welfare agency (e.g. ER.GO, DiscoLazio, Edisu).
Italian Language Course Standard (Free CLA) Free intensive and semester Italian language courses offered through University Language Centers (CLA) and municipal CPIA schools.
Peer Buddy Mentorship Standard Peer Network Personal pairing with Italian and senior refugee student mentors, plus UNHCR Community Matching in 13 Italian cities.

Legal & Immigration Orientation Notice

Academic Success

Italian University Academic Survival Guide

Mastering the Italian university credit system (CFU), exam grading, oral exams, and university administration.

The CFU Credit System

CREDITI FORMATIVI UNIVERSITARI

1 CFU equals 25 hours of total student workload (lectures + individual study). A full academic year consists of 60 CFU (120 CFU total for a 2-year Master's degree).

  • Scholarship Renewal: Requires earning 30–36 CFU by August 10th of your first year
  • Equivalent to the European Credit Transfer System (ECTS)

Exam Grading (18–30 e Lode)

APPELLI & SESSIONS

Grades range from 0 to 30. The minimum passing mark is 18/30, and the maximum with honors is 30L (30 e Lode).

  • Right to Reject: You have the right to accept or reject (rifiutare) a grade and retake the exam
  • 3 Exam Sessions: Winter (Jan–Feb), Summer (June–July), Autumn (Sept)

Oral Exams (Esame Orale)

EXAM FORMAT

Unlike multiple-choice systems, most Italian university courses include an in-depth oral examination conducted directly with the professor.

  • Practice conceptual explanations and key theories out loud
  • Attend public oral exams of peers beforehand to understand professor questions
Campus Terminology

Essential Italian University Glossary

Appello Esami
An official exam session date. Courses typically offer 2 to 3 appelli per exam session. You must register in advance on your student portal.
Verbalizzazione Segreteria
The official digital recording of your accepted exam grade into the university database and your online student booklet (Libretto Universitario).
Ricevimento Docenti
Dedicated office consultation hours where professors meet students one-on-one for thesis guidance, course questions, and exam clarifications.
ISEE Parificato Borse di Studio
The official equivalent economic indicator document for foreign students, required by regional bodies (DSU/EDiSU) for student fee benefits and services.
Mensa Universitaria Servizi
Subsidized university dining hall providing hot balanced meals at student discount rates accessible with your student badge or app.
Tassa Regionale Tasse
The regional fee for the right to higher education (typically ~€140/year), often fully reimbursed or waived under UNICORE scholarship agreements.
Post-Graduation Bridge

From Thesis to Career: Alumni Transition & Inclusion Guide

Legal residence conversion, corporate inclusion gateways, municipal integration hubs, and doctoral Ph.D. pathways upon completing your Master's degree.

12-Month Job Search Permit

ART. 39-BIS.1 D.LGS. 286/98

Under Italian immigration law, graduates of an Italian Master's degree can convert their study permit into a 12-Month Permesso per Ricerca Lavoro o Imprenditorialità.

  • Register with the public Employment Center (Centro per l'Impiego - CPI)
  • Submit conversion Kit Giallo before your student permit expires
  • Convert directly into a Work Permit (Lavoro Subordinato) once hired

Welcome-in-One-Click Gateway

UNHCR • 500+ EMPLOYERS

Official job gateway with 50,000+ activated pathways connecting refugee graduates with certified Italian companies, multinationals, and research organizations.

  • Step-by-step CV builder with confidential permit verification by UNHCR staff
  • Direct applications to corporate jobs & traineeships at welcomeclick.unhcr.it
  • Support contact: info.welcome@unhcr.org

Community Matching & Spazio Comune

13 CITIES • MUNICIPAL DESKS

Pair with local professionals in 13 cities (Rome, Milan, Bologna, Turin, Naples, etc.) and access one-stop municipal integration desks in 7 major cities.

  • 1,500+ mentor matches for career networking and local integration
  • Walk-in support for civil registry, housing inclusion, and social orientation
  • Contact UNHCR integration team: integrazione@unhcr.org

Assolavoro Welfare & Training

GRANTS & REIMBURSEMENTS

Social aid allowances and educational support funded through employment agencies under the national Assolavoro & UNHCR agreement.

  • Financial subsidies for university textbooks, study materials, and tuition fees
  • 100% Reimbursement for psychological therapy and mental health sessions
  • Nursery support (Asilo Nido) and free certified professional training courses

Doctoral (Ph.D.) Research

DOTTORATO DI RICERCA

Graduates with an accredited Italian Laurea Magistrale can compete for fully funded 3-year Ph.D. scholarships (€1,200–€1,600/month net) across Italian and European universities.

  • Annual public competitions (Bandi di Dottorato published May–July)
  • Proven alumni placements at CNR Pisa, Univ. Parma, Univ. Bergamo, and TU Chemnitz
