Mission & Vision

What is University Corridors for Refugees?

A flagship higher education initiative offering refugee youth safe, legal, and fully-funded pathways to complete Master's degree studies across Italy.

Purpose

Bridging the Global Refugee Education Gap

Enabling displaced academic talent to rebuild their futures with dignity and scholarship excellence.

From Displacement to Higher Education

The University Corridors for Refugees (UNICORE) project was established to allow refugees residing in countries of first asylum to continue their higher education in Italy, completing two-year Master’s degree programs (*Laurea Magistrale*).

Instead of facing hazardous irregular journeys, refugee scholars enter Italy with regular National Study Visas (Type D), received with full tuition fee waivers, monthly living stipends, accommodation, and community integration support.

The project is promoted by 40+ Italian universities in close collaboration with the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR), the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MAECI), Caritas Italiana, Diaconia Valdese, Centro Astalli, and local reception networks.

The UNHCR 15by30 Target

Globally, only 7% of refugee youth have access to higher education, compared to the global youth average of over 40%.

15% by 2030
UNHCR’s global target to expand tertiary enrollment for young refugees through complementary university pathways.
UNICORE is celebrated across Europe as a pioneer model for legal higher education corridors.
Foundations

The 3 Pillars of UNICORE

Every scholarship under UNICORE is built upon three non-negotiable principles of dignity and academic quality.

1. Safe & Legal Entry

Eliminates dangerous, life-threatening crossings. Scholars travel to Italy with national study visas facilitated by the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MAECI) and UNHCR.

2. Comprehensive Funding

Full scholarship coverage: complete tuition fee waivers, monthly living stipends, student accommodation, study materials allowance, and health insurance.

3. Holistic Community Inclusion

Beyond academics, students receive psychosocial support, peer "Buddy" mentorship from Italian students, Italian language courses, and administrative guidance for permits.

Partner Matrix

How UNICORE is Governed & Organised

A multi-stakeholder partnership uniting Italian universities, UN agencies, government ministries, and humanitarian organizations.

Academia

40+ Italian Universities

Autonomous selection of candidates, issuance of admission letters, full tuition fee waivers, and academic department tutoring.

UN Agency

UNHCR Italia

International coordination, refugee status verification with country of asylum offices, policy standards, and global advocacy.

Government

MAECI (Foreign Affairs)

Diplomatic support, consular visa coordination (Type D Study Visa), and facilitation through Italian embassies overseas.

Humanitarian

Caritas Italiana

Accommodation arrangements, welcome kits, food and living support, and local integration networks across Italian dioceses.

Psychosocial

Diaconia Valdese

Mental health support, psychological counseling, trauma care, community centers, and cultural orientation sessions.

Legal

Centro Astalli (JRS Italy)

Legal clinics, immigration law consultations, Permesso di Soggiorno assistance, and guidance for post-graduation status transition.
