Overview & Mission

What is University Corridors for Refugees?

Discover the vision, humanitarian principles, institutional partnership matrix, and global targets driving the UNICORE initiative.

The Vision

A Safe, Dignified Academic Bridge to Higher Education

UNICORE represents one of Europe's most successful complementary education pathways for displaced youth.

Transforming Talent into Global Leadership

The University Corridors for Refugees (UNICORE) project was established to allow refugees residing in first countries of asylum to continue their higher education in Italy, completing two-year Master’s degree programs (*Laurea Magistrale*).

Instead of risking life-threatening irregular routes, refugee scholars obtain regular National Study Visas (Type D), fly directly to Italy with full financial support, and integrate into university communities with dedicated local partners.

The project is promoted by Italian universities in close collaboration with the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR), the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation (MAECI), Caritas Italiana, Diaconia Valdese, Centro Astalli, and local reception networks.

The UNHCR 15by30 Target

Worldwide, only 7% of young refugees have access to higher education, compared to the global youth average of 40%+.

15% by 2030
UNHCR’s global goal to enroll 15% of refugee youth in tertiary education by 2030 through initiatives like UNICORE.
UNICORE serves as an international benchmark model for higher education complementary pathways across the EU.
Fundamental Principles

The 3 Pillars of the UNICORE Model

Every scholarship under UNICORE is built upon three non-negotiable foundations of dignity and quality.

1. Safe & Legal Entry

Eliminates dangerous, life-threatening irregular crossings. Scholars travel to Italy with valid national study visas and passport waivers facilitated by the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and UNHCR.

2. Full Financial & Academic Coverage

Complete scholarship coverage guarantees that financial barriers are removed: full tuition waiver, monthly stipend, university housing, study materials, and health insurance.

3. Holistic Social Inclusion

Beyond academics, students receive psychosocial support, peer "Buddy" mentorship from Italian students, Italian language courses, and administrative accompaniment for legal permits.

Multistakeholder Model

How UNICORE is Governed & Organised

A unique coalition uniting academia, humanitarian agencies, government ministries, and civil society.

Academia

40+ Italian Universities

Autonomous selection of candidates, issuance of admission letters, full tuition fee waivers, and academic department tutoring.

UN Agency

UNHCR (The UN Refugee Agency)

International coordination, refugee status verification with country of asylum offices, policy standards, and global advocacy.

Government

MAECI (Ministry of Foreign Affairs)

Diplomatic support, consular visa coordination (Type D Study Visa), and facilitation through Italian embassies overseas.

Humanitarian Partner

Caritas Italiana

Accommodation arrangements, welcome kits, food and living support, and local integration network across Italian dioceses.

Psychosocial Care

Diaconia Valdese

Mental health support, psychological counseling, trauma support, community centers, and cultural orientation sessions.

Legal Support

Centro Astalli (JRS Italy)

Legal clinics, immigration law consultations, Permesso di Soggiorno assistance, and guidance for post-graduation status transition.
