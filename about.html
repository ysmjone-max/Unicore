Institutional Background

What is the UNICORE Project?

University Corridors for Refugees (UNICORE) is an Italian multi-stakeholder partnership offering regular higher education pathways for refugee students living in asylum countries.

The 15by30 Target

Transforming Refugee Higher Education

Globally, only 7% of refugee youth have access to tertiary higher education, compared to the global average of over 42%. The UNHCR 15by30 roadmap aims to achieve 15% enrollment of refugees in higher education by the year 2030.

Initiated in 2019 by the University of Bologna and Luiss University in coordination with UNHCR, the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MAECI), and civil society partners, UNICORE offers a safe legal alternative to irregular journeys by recognizing academic merit.

Strategic Framework

The 3 Pillars of UNICORE

The program integrates academic opportunity, legal regular pathways, and long-term socio-economic inclusion.

1. Safe & Regular Entry

Enables students to enter Italy safely with regular study visas, bypassing hazardous irregular migration routes and securing documented legal residency.

2. Comprehensive Funding

Provides full tuition fee waivers, monthly living allowances, accommodation, local transportation, and initial arrival stipends throughout the degree.

3. Holistic Social Inclusion

Integrates scholars into campus life through peer buddy mentorship, intensive Italian language courses, psychological counseling, and legal support.

Clarity & Accountability

Who Does What? Partner Responsibility Guide

To ensure total transparency and resolve student confusion, here is the official division of roles and duties across all UNICORE project partners.

UNHCR (UN Refugee Agency)

STRATEGIC & PROTECTION
  • Overall project coordination, bando guidelines, and country-of-asylum identification
  • Verification of refugee status and pre-departure travel document liaisons
  • Post-graduation integration frameworks: Welcome-in-One-Click, Community Matching, Spazio Comune

Participating Universities

ACADEMIC & ENROLLMENT
  • Publication of university bandi, academic screening, and interview selection
  • 100% Tuition fee waivers and exemption from university registration fees
  • Academic faculty tutors, Italian CLA language courses, and student housing desk
  • Free on-campus psychological counseling (Sportello di Ascolto)

Caritas, Diaconia Valdese & Centro Astalli

LOCAL WELFARE & ACCOMPANIMENT
  • Disbursement of monthly living allowances / scholarship maintenance stipends
  • Arrival reception, airport pickup, and temporary housing allocation
  • In-person bureaucratic accompaniment for Kit Giallo, Codice Fiscale, ASL, and Bank accounts
  • Social canteens, food gift cards, and community integration activities

MAECI (Ministry of Foreign Affairs)

VISAS & CONSULAR PROTOCOL
  • Facilitation of Italian National Type D Study Visas via embassies and consulates
  • Special consular coordination for refugee scholars lacking conventional passports
