PHASE 0 • BEFORE LEAVING (T-MINUS 30 DAYS)

You're Selected! Pre-Departure Preparation

👋
Hi, I'm Sami! I came to Italy through UNICORE too. When I received my university admission letter, I was dancing with joy—and then immediately froze: "What paperwork do I need before boarding? What if I forget something in my asylum camp or city?" Take a deep breath. Let's get your pre-departure organized step by step! 🇮🇹

1. What is this step?

The transition window between university selection and boarding your flight to Italy. This is when Italian Embassies, UNHCR field offices, and your host university coordinate your Italian Study Visa (Type D) or Consular Emergency Laissez-Passer.

2. Why does it matter?

Once you board the plane, replacing an original degree certificate, refugee mandate letter, or high school transcript from your country of first asylum is extremely difficult and can delay your university enrollment for months.

3. Interactive Pre-Departure Checklist (Click to complete):

Sami's Golden Rule: NEVER put documents in checked luggage!

Always carry all original certificates, passports, visa letters, and medical prescriptions in your backpack / hand luggage. Checked baggage can be delayed, but your documents must stay with you at all border control checkpoints.

Who can help you at this stage?

Your local UNHCR country office, host university International Student Desk, and your assigned UNICORE Student Buddy (reach out on WhatsApp!).

Packing Smart & Preparing for Italian Seasons

😅
I remember staring at my empty suitcase thinking: "Do I pack traditional spices? How cold is Italy in October?" Trust me: Italian autumn starts getting chilly in October (down to 5°C–12°C in northern/central cities like Milan, Turin, Bologna, Florence, and Verona!).

1. What is this step?

Packing your luggage smartly for the climate, electronics compatibility, and the emotional transition of moving to Italy.

2. Why does it matter?

Italian wall outlets use Type C and Type L (round 2 or 3 pins). If your phone or laptop charger has flat US blades or bulky UK square pins, you won't be able to charge your phone upon landing without an adapter!

3. Essential Packing Checklist:

Customs Warning: Don't pack unpackaged food!

EU border customs will confiscate unpackaged meat, dairy, or fresh agricultural products. Bring sealed dry spices or sentimental memories instead, and buy fresh groceries locally in Italy.

PHASE 1 • BUREAUCRACY (DAYS 3–7)

The Italian Documents: Codice Fiscale & Kit Giallo

📑
"Welcome to Italy... where one document unlocks the next document!" 😅 It looks complicated, but it's just a simple 4-step sequence:
1. PASSPORT & VISAYour legal entry ticket into Italy
2. CODICE FISCALEItalian Tax Code (Agenzia delle Entrate - Day 3)
3. KIT GIALLO (POSTE ITALIANE)Residence permit application (Within 8 working days)
4. RICEVUTA POSTALEYour legal shield while waiting for Questura

1. Codice Fiscale (Tax Code)

Issued free at the Agenzia delle Entrate (or pre-issued by the Italian Embassy). You need it for bank accounts, SIM cards, university enrollment, and apartment contracts.

2. Kit Giallo & Permesso di Soggiorno

Pick up the free yellow envelope (*Kit Giallo*) at any Post Office *Sportello Amico*. Fill out Modulo 1 with your buddy. Submit it at the post office counter. Pay the postal fee (~€118–€148) and collect your Ricevuta Postale (Postal Receipt).

The Ricevuta is your legal shield!

While waiting for your Questura fingerprinting appointment (which might take several months), your official postal receipt (Ricevuta) carries 100% legal validity to live, study, work up to 20h/week, and access healthcare in Italy!

PHASE 1 • CAMPUS LIFE (DAYS 5–10)

Welcome to University: The 4 Doors

🏛️
"Congratulations! You are officially a Master's student at an Italian university." When you step onto campus, remember these Four Main Doors:
Door 1

International Student Desk

Your primary home for UNICORE orientation, scholarship questions, late arrival waivers, and tutor pairing.

Door 2

Segreteria Studenti (Registrar)

Handles official student card (Badge), enrollment certificates, exam booking portal (Esse3/Gomp), and CFU credits.

Door 3

Programme Coordinator (Professors)

Course curriculum, exam syllabus, laboratory placements, and Master's thesis supervisor assignment.

Door 4

Career & Placement Office

Curriculum workshops, curricular internships, company job fairs, and Erasmus+ exchange mobility bandi.
