Support Directory & Useful Connections

A comprehensive, verified directory of specialized partner organizations providing accommodation, psychological support, legal clinics, academic recognition, and emergency help in Italy.

Housing & Welfare

Caritas Italiana

Provides vital housing assistance, welcome kits, food subsidies, and diocesan community networks that welcome UNICORE scholars upon arrival in Italian cities.

  • Student accommodation arrangements
  • Meal subsidies and food distribution centers
  • Local family integration networks
National Network Visit Caritas
Psychosocial Care

Diaconia Valdese (CSD)

Specialized in psychosocial well-being, trauma-informed counseling, cultural orientation, and community centers (*Community Center*) across Italy.

  • Free psychological support & mental healthcare
  • Cultural orientation & social inclusion workshops
  • Peer counseling and support circles
Community Care Visit Diaconia
Legal Clinics

Centro Astalli (JRS Italy)

The Italian branch of Jesuit Refugee Service, offering specialized legal orientation, Permesso di Soggiorno assistance, and guidance for international protection rights.

  • Immigration legal counsel & Questura guidance
  • Permesso di Soggiorno Studio to Work conversion
  • Italian language learning laboratories
Legal Aid Visit Centro Astalli
Academic Recognition

CIMEA & EQPR Service

The official Italian Information Centre on Academic Mobility and Equivalence. Issues Statements of Comparability and the European Qualifications Passport for Refugees (EQPR).

  • Verification of foreign undergraduate degrees
  • Statement of Comparability / Verification
  • Credential assessment for missing documentation
Credentials Authority Visit CIMEA
Community & Culture

ARCI Nazionale

Nationwide cultural association running social clubs, language exchanges, and free helpline support (*Numero Verde per Richiedenti Asilo e Rifugiati*).

  • Free Helpline for Refugees: 800 905 570
  • Cultural events, student meetups, and language exchanges
  • Local community circles across all Italian regions
Cultural Network Visit ARCI
Emergency Lines

Emergency Helplines in Italy

Essential toll-free emergency phone numbers to know when living and studying in Italy:

  • 112 – Single European Emergency Number (Police / Carabinieri)
  • 118 – Medical Emergency & Ambulance Service (*Pronto Soccorso*)
  • 1522 – Anti-Violence & Stalking Helpline (Multilingual, 24/7)
  • 800 905 570 – Toll-Free Refugee Helpline (ARCI/UNHCR)
