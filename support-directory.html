Government

MAECI & Italian Embassies

The Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation coordinates with Italian embassies across the 13 eligible countries to issue National Study Visas (Type D) for selected scholars.

  • Study visa regulations & requirements
  • Italian consular network directory
  • Official bilateral mobility protocols

Housing & Reception

Caritas Italiana

A key national partner coordinating local diocesan networks in every university city to guarantee accommodation, welcome packages, community mentorship, and immediate living assistance.

  • Dormitory & apartment placement
  • Local integration & volunteer networks
  • Emergency social assistance
National Partner Visit Caritas
Psychosocial Care

Diaconia Valdese

Provides specialized mental health support, trauma-informed psychological counseling, cultural transition assistance, and community drop-in centers across Italy.

  • Free confidential psychological therapy
  • Cultural orientation & coping strategies
  • Community spaces & intercultural workshops
Healthcare & Psychosocial Visit Diaconia
Legal Clinic

Centro Astalli (JRS Italy)

The Italian branch of Jesuit Refugee Service, offering dedicated legal advice on immigration laws, *Permesso di Soggiorno* renewals, tax code issues, and transition to post-study work permits.

  • Immigration & residence permit guidance
  • Free legal clinic appointments
  • Post-graduation permit conversion advice
Legal Support Network Visit Astalli
Academic Recognition

CIMEA & Universitaly

The official Italian center for academic recognition (MUR). Issues Statements of Comparability and implements the European Qualifications Passport for Refugees (EQPR) for missing documentation.

  • Statements of Comparability & Verification
  • European Qualifications Passport (EQPR)
  • Official Italian pre-enrollment portal
