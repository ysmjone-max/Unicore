Interactive Modulo 1 Field Guide (Pages 1–3)

Switch between tabs below to view annotated fields corresponding to each page of the official Italian postal kit.

Header & Location
Sigla Provincia & Comune SI / RM / BO / MI

Write the 2-letter abbreviation of your university town (e.g. SI for Siena, RM for Rome, BO for Bologna, TO for Turin) and write the full municipality name in capital letters.

Top-Right Box
Duty Stamp (Marca da Bollo) €16.00

Stick the €16.00 tax revenue stamp (purchased at any Tabaccheria) directly into the designated square box on page 1.

Type of Request
Rilascio vs Rinnovo RILASCIO [X]

Tick "Rilascio" for your first residence permit upon arrival in Italy. Tick "Rinnovo" for subsequent annual scholarship renewals.

Box 14 & 21
Reason Code & Attachments Count CODICE: 11

Box 14: Code 11 (Studio / Study).
Box 21: Total number of attached photocopy sheets (Passport, Visa, University Enrollment, Scholarship Guarantee, Insurance).

Bottom of Page 1
Date of Submission & Signature DO NOT SIGN YET!

Write the date of submission when you go to the post office. DO NOT sign at home — you must sign in front of the postal operator at Sportello Amico!

Sezione 1
Codice Fiscale & Personal Data 16 CHARACTERS

Enter your Italian Tax Code (if already obtained from Agenzia delle Entrate).
Stato Civile: A (Single / Celibe-Nubile) or B (Married).
Sesso: M or F | Data di Nascita: DD / MM / YYYY.

Sezione 2
Passport / Travel Document 01 = GOV / 06 = POLICE

Documento: 01 (Passport) or 02 (Refugee CTD).
Rilasciato da: 01 (Government) or 06 (Police authority).
Enter Passport Number and Expiration Date.

Sezione 2 (Entry Stamp)
Date & Port of Entry in Italy FIUMICINO / MALPENSA

Check the Italian border police ink stamp inside your passport:
Data di ingresso: Exact arrival date.
Valico di frontiera: e.g. AEROPORTO FIUMICINO ROMA or AEROPORTO MALPENSA MILANO.

Sezione 2 (Visa Details)
Visa Number, Type & Validity TYPE: D | STUDIO

Numero Visto: Visa number from the vignette sticker.
Tipo Visto: D (National Study Visa).
Motivo: STUDIO.
Enter days of validity, start date (Valido dal), and end date (Valido al).

Sezione 3 (Residence in Italy)
Personal Italian Address STREET & NUMBER

Sigla Provincia & Comune: e.g. SI / SIENA.
Indirizzo: Street name (e.g. VIA SAN VIGILIO), building number (6), and Italian postal code (53100).

Sezione 3 (University Reference)
Presso (C/O International Relations) UNIVERSITY DESK

If instructed by your host university, write the official department reference under Presso (e.g. DIVISIONE RELAZIONI INTERNAZIONALI UNIVERSITA DI SIENA) to ensure Questura correspondence reaches your international coordinator.

Sezione 3 (Direct Contact)
Italian Mobile & Email 2FA & SMS ALERTS

Write your active Italian phone number and institutional university email address. The Questura uses this mobile number to send SMS notifications when your electronic card is ready.

Modulo 2 Rule
Modulo 2 (Employment Section) LEAVE BLANK

DO NOT fill out Modulo 2! Modulo 2 is strictly reserved for applicants with subordinate employment contracts or business ownership.

1
Step 1: Pick Up the Envelope

Collect the Kit Giallo at Poste Italiane

Visit any post office branch featuring a Sportello Amico counter and ask: "Vorrei un kit per il permesso di soggiorno per favore". The kit envelope is 100% free of charge.

  • Free yellow-striped envelope (Kit Giallo)
  • Modulo 1 (Personal data form)
  • Pre-printed postal bulletin (Bollettino Postale 674)
2
Step 2: Prepare Photocopies

Prepare Required Photocopies (Never Originals)

Insert only clear A4 photocopies into the envelope. Keep all original documents with you:

  • Photocopy of all pages of your Passport & Italian Type D Visa
  • Photocopy of University Enrollment Certificate (Iscrizione Universitaria)
  • Photocopy of UNICORE Scholarship Award Letter / Financial Guarantee
  • Photocopy of Health Insurance / Tessera Sanitaria registration
  • 1x Revenue Stamp (Marca da Bollo) of €16.00 (affixed to top-right of page 1)
3
Step 3: Submission & Counter Payment

Submitting the Kit at Sportello Amico

Take the open envelope and your original passport to a Poste Italiane Sportello Amico counter within 8 working days of arrival:

  • €70.46 (or €30.46) Electronic Permit Production Fee via pre-printed bulletin
  • €30.00 Postal Service Fee paid directly at the counter
  • Receive the official Ricevuta Postale (Cedolino) with User ID and Password
  • Receive the Questura appointment slip with date, time, and address for fingerprinting
4
Step 4: Biometrics at Questura

Questura Appointment (Fotosegnalamento)

Attend your appointment at the local Police Immigration Office (Ufficio Immigrazione della Questura):

  • Bring 4 passport-size photos (identical, white background, recent)
  • Bring original Passport, original Ricevuta Postale, and university certificates
  • Biometric fingerprinting (Rilevamento impronte digitali)
5
Step 5: Online Status Tracking

Tracking Your Permit Online

Track the progress of your electronic card using the credentials on your postal receipt:

  • portaleimmigrazione.it: Enter User ID & Password (found on your postal receipt)
  • poliziadistato.it: Enter the 10-digit registered mail number (Numero Assicurata)
  • You will receive an SMS when the electronic card is ready for collection at the Questura
Civil Registry & Services

Core Italian Civil & Health Documents

Key bureaucratic steps after submitting your residence permit application.

Codice Fiscale

AGENZIA DELLE ENTRATE

A 16-character alphanumeric tax identifier mandatory for all legal, university, financial, and healthcare activities in Italy.

  • Free of charge at any local Agenzia delle Entrate office
  • Bring original passport, visa, and university certificate

Carta d'Identità Elettronica (CIE)

COMUNE / ANAGRAFE

Electronic National Identity Card issued by your city hall (Comune) after completing residence registration (Iscrizione Anagrafica / Residenza).

  • Valid photo ID for travel within Italy and the Schengen area
  • Cost: ~€22.20; valid for 10 years

Tessera Sanitaria & Medico di Base

ASL (LOCAL HEALTH AUTHORITY)

National Health Card granting free access to a General Practitioner (Medico di Base), emergency care, and subsidized prescriptions across Italy.

  • Register at the nearest territorial ASL health desk
  • Free general doctor visits, referrals, and medical certificates
Practical Italian

Essential Words & Phrases for Daily Life & Offices

Practical vocabulary and spoken Italian phrases to help you communicate confidently in public offices and around town.

Permesso di Soggiorno Questura
Meaning: Residence Permit.
"Ho l'appuntamento per il fotosegnalamento e le impronte digitali." (I have an appointment for photos and fingerprints.)
Marca da Bollo Tabaccheria
Meaning: Official tax revenue stamp (€16.00).
"Vorrei una marca da bollo da sedici euro per favore." (I would like a €16 revenue stamp please.)
Ricevuta Postale Poste Italiane
Meaning: Postal receipt (Cedolino).
Legal temporary document proving your lawful stay in Italy while awaiting the physical plastic permit.
Iscrizione Anagrafica Comune
Meaning: Civil registry residence registration.
"Vorrei richiedere la residenza e la carta d'identità." (I would like to apply for residency and ID card.)
Medico di Base Salute (ASL)
Meaning: Family doctor / General Practitioner.
"Vorrei fare la scelta del medico di base per favore." (I would like to register with a family doctor.)
Segreteria Studenti Università
Meaning: Student Administrative Registry Office.
"Devo ritirare il certificato di iscrizione per la Questura." (I need to collect the enrollment certificate for Questura.)
Contratto di Locazione Alloggio
Meaning: Registered rental lease agreement.
Essential document proving your official accommodation for residence and Questura applications.
Abbonamento Mezzi Trasporti
Meaning: Public transit subscription pass.
"Vorrei fare l'abbonamento mensile/annuale studenti." (I would like to purchase the student monthly/annual transit pass.)
