Official Procedure

Step-by-Step Kit Giallo Filling Guide

How to correctly complete and submit the residence permit application (Kit Giallo) at Poste Italiane.

1
Step 1: Pick Up the Envelope

Collect the Kit Giallo at Poste Italiane

Visit any major post office branch featuring a Sportello Amico desk and ask the postal clerk: "Vorrei un kit per il permesso di soggiorno per favore". The kit envelope is completely free of charge.

  • Free yellow-striped envelope (Kit Giallo)
  • Contains Modulo 1 (Personal data) and Modulo 2 (Work data - leave blank)
  • Contains the postal payment slip (Bollettino postale)
2
Step 2: Complete Modulo 1

Filling Out Modulo 1 (Student Instructions)

Fill out Modulo 1 ONLY using a BLACK ink pen and writing in clear CAPITAL LETTERS within the designated boxes:

  • Sezione 1: Select "Rilascio" (first issuance) and write code 11 for Motivo: Studio (Study).
  • Dati Anagrafici: Surname (Cognome), Given Name (Nome), Date of Birth, Gender, Nationality.
  • Dati Passaporto: Passport/Travel document number, expiry date, issuing authority.
  • Recapito in Italia: Address of your university dormitory or temporary apartment in Italy.
  • DO NOT SIGN the form yet — you will sign it in front of the postal operator!
3
Step 3: Document Attachments

Prepare Required Photocopies (Never Originals)

Insert only clear A4 photocopies into the envelope. Keep all original documents with you:

  • Photocopy of all pages of your Passport & Italian Type D Visa
  • Photocopy of University Enrollment Certificate (Iscrizione Universitaria)
  • Photocopy of UNICORE Scholarship Award Letter / Financial Guarantee
  • Photocopy of Health Insurance / Tessera Sanitaria registration
  • 1x Revenue Stamp (Marca da Bollo) of €16.00 purchased from a Tobacco shop (Tabaccheria)
4
Step 4: Submission & Payment

Submitting the Kit at Sportello Amico

Take the open envelope and your original passport to a Poste Italiane Sportello Amico. The postal operator will verify your passport, affix the postal bar code, and process your payments:

  • €70.46 (or €30.46 for 1-year) Electronic Permit Production Fee via pre-printed bulletin
  • €30.00 Postal Service Fee paid directly at the counter
  • Receive the official Ricevuta Postale (Cedolino) with User ID and Password
  • Receive appointment slip with exact date, time, and Questura address for fingerprinting
5
Step 5: Biometrics & Tracking

Questura Appointment & Online SMS Tracking

Attend your appointment at the local Police Immigration Office (Ufficio Immigrazione della Questura):

  • Bring 4 passport-size photos (white background, recent)
  • Bring original Passport, original Ricevuta Postale, and university certificates
  • Biometric fingerprinting (Rilevamento impronte digitali)
  • Track permit status online at portaleimmigrazione.it or poliziadistato.it
Civil Registry & Services

Core Italian Civil & Health Documents

Key bureaucratic steps after submitting your residence permit application.

Codice Fiscale

AGENZIA DELLE ENTRATE

A 16-character alphanumeric tax identifier mandatory for all legal, university, financial, and healthcare activities in Italy.

  • Free of charge at any local Agenzia delle Entrate office
  • Bring original passport, visa, and university certificate

Carta d'Identità Elettronica (CIE)

COMUNE / ANAGRAFE

Electronic National Identity Card issued by your city hall (Comune) after completing residence registration (Iscrizione Anagrafica / Residenza).

  • Valid photo ID for travel within Italy and the Schengen area
  • Cost: ~€22.20; valid for 10 years

Tessera Sanitaria & Medico di Base

ASL (LOCAL HEALTH AUTHORITY)

National Health Card granting free access to a General Practitioner (Medico di Base), emergency care, and subsidized prescriptions across Italy.

  • Register at the nearest territorial ASL health desk
  • Free general doctor visits, referrals, and medical certificates
Practical Italian

Essential Words & Phrases for Daily Life & Offices

Practical vocabulary and spoken Italian phrases to help you communicate confidently in public offices and around town.

Permesso di Soggiorno Questura
Meaning: Residence Permit.
"Ho l'appuntamento per il fotosegnalamento e le impronte digitali." (I have an appointment for photos and fingerprints.)
Marca da Bollo Tabaccheria
Meaning: Official tax revenue stamp (€16.00).
"Vorrei una marca da bollo da sedici euro per favore." (I would like a €16 revenue stamp please.)
Ricevuta Postale Poste Italiane
Meaning: Postal receipt (Cedolino).
Legal temporary document proving your lawful stay in Italy while awaiting the physical plastic permit.
Iscrizione Anagrafica Comune
Meaning: Civil registry residence registration.
"Vorrei richiedere la residenza e la carta d'identità." (I would like to apply for residency and ID card.)
Medico di Base Salute (ASL)
Meaning: Family doctor / General Practitioner.
"Vorrei fare la scelta del medico di base per favore." (I would like to register with a family doctor.)
Segreteria Studenti Università
Meaning: Student Administrative Registry Office.
"Devo ritirare il certificato di iscrizione per la Questura." (I need to collect the enrollment certificate for Questura.)
Contratto di Locazione Alloggio
Meaning: Registered rental lease agreement.
Essential document proving your official accommodation for residence and Questura applications.
Abbonamento Mezzi Trasporti
Meaning: Public transit subscription pass.
"Vorrei fare l'abbonamento mensile/annuale studenti." (I would like to purchase the student monthly/annual transit pass.)
