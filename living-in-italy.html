Official Form Guide

Modulo 1 & Kit Giallo: Step-by-Step Instructions & PDF Preview

Click the dropdown below (+ / −) to explore the complete box-by-box filling procedure, attachment rules, and interactive embedded PDF preview.

1

Procure the Yellow Envelope (Kit Giallo) & €16 Marca da Bollo

Go to any post office branch with an active Sportello Amico desk and ask the postal clerk: "Vorrei un kit per il permesso di soggiorno, per favore." The envelope is 100% free of charge. Then, visit any Tobacco shop (Tabaccheria displaying the white "T" sign) and buy a €16.00 Marca da Bollo revenue stamp.

  • Stick the €16.00 stamp directly in the designated rectangular box on the top-right corner of Page 1.
  • Write exclusively in BLACK INK and CAPITAL LETTERS (STAMPATELLO MAIUSCOLO).
2

Box-by-Box Guide: Completing Modulo 1 (Pages 1, 2 & 3)

Only fill MODULO 1. (DO NOT touch Modulo 2 — it is strictly for employed workers and must be left entirely blank).

  • Page 1 – Boxes 1 & 2 (Province & City): Write your university's 2-letter province abbreviation (e.g. SI for Siena, RM for Rome, BO for Bologna, MI for Milan) and the full city name.
  • Page 1 – Box 3 (Request Type): Put an [X] in RILASCIO for your first permit upon arrival, or RINNOVO for subsequent years.
  • Page 1 – Boxes 14 & 21 (Reason & Attachments): Write Reason Code 11 (Studio) in Box 14. Write total count of attached photocopies (e.g. 08) in Box 21.
  • Page 1 – Signature: DO NOT sign Page 1 at home! You must physically sign in front of the postal operator at Sportello Amico.
  • Page 2 – Tax Code & Civil Status: Enter your 16-character Codice Fiscale. Mark marital status (A = Single, B = Married) and Sex (M / F).
  • Page 2 – Passport, Border Entry & Visa Details: Document type 01 (Passport), passport number, expiration date. Copy the Italian Border Police stamp (Port of Entry e.g. Aeroporto Fiumicino Roma and date). Copy Type D visa number and validity dates.
  • Page 3 – Italian Domicile & University C/O: Write your Italian street address, house number, and CAP postal code. Under Presso, write your university international relations division (e.g. C/O WELCOME DESK UNIVERSITA) if instructed. Write your Italian mobile number for SMS alerts.
3

Prepare the Required A4 Photocopies (Never Send Originals!)

Insert ONLY clean A4 photocopies into the yellow envelope. Keep all original passports and certificates safely with you to present in person at the Questura.

  • 1x Copy of Passport pages with biographical data, Italian Type D Visa, and border entry ink stamp.
  • 1x Copy of UNICORE Scholarship Award Letter & university pre-enrollment certificate.
  • 1x Copy of Italian Codice Fiscale certificate (from Agenzia delle Entrate).
  • 1x Copy of health insurance policy / enrollment receipt.
  • 1x Copy of housing contract or registered hospitality declaration (Dichiarazione di Ospitalità).
4

Pay the Postal Bulletin & Submit at Sportello Amico (Day 8 Deadline)

Take the open yellow envelope to a Poste Italiane Sportello Amico counter within 8 business days of arriving in Italy.

  • Pay the pre-printed postal payment slip (Bollettino 674) for €70.46 (electronic permit card fee).
  • Pay the insured registered post fee of €30.00 at the counter.
  • Sign Page 1 of Modulo 1 in front of the postal operator.
  • Receive the Ricevuta Postale (Cedolino) containing your User ID, Password, and your official Questura Fingerprinting Appointment Date.

Core Legal Knowledge Base

Essential Bureaucratic Concepts & Documents

Click on any document below (+ / −) for an in-depth breakdown: what it is, why it is critical for refugee scholars, what you must know, and exact procedural costs.

Definition
What is it? (Che cos'è?)

The official plastic electronic identity card granting non-EU citizens the legal right to reside in Italy. While your Type D Visa allowed you to enter Italy once, the Permesso di Soggiorno is what legally authorizes your stay for the full academic duration.

Legal Purpose
Why is it important? (Perché è fondamentale?)

Without it (or its postal receipt Ricevuta), you become irregular under Italian immigration law. It is required to maintain university matriculation, sign apartment leases, open bank accounts, access healthcare, and travel.

What the Student MUST Know (Rules & Legal Rights)
Mandatory Guidelines
8-Day Rule

You must submit the postal kit (Kit Giallo) at a post office within 8 working days of stepping foot in Italy.

Work Rights

Legally authorizes working part-time up to 20 hours/week (max 1,040 hours/year).

Annual Renewal

Renewal requires passing at least 1 exam in year 1, and 2 exams (approx. 30–36 CFU) in subsequent years.

Ricevuta Shield

The postal receipt acts as legal proof of regular residence while the electronic card is being manufactured.

Official Cost €116.46 (€70.46 card + €30 post + €16 stamp)
Competent Authority Poste Italiane (Sportello Amico) → Questura
Definition
What is it? (Che cos'è?)

A 16-character alphanumeric code uniquely identifying every individual in relations with Italian public bodies, administration, and financial institutions. It is calculated based on your surname, first name, date of birth, gender, and country of birth.

Legal Purpose
Why is it important? (Perché è fondamentale?)

It is the universal key to everyday life in Italy. You cannot sign a housing lease, buy an Italian SIM card, open a bank account, receive UNICORE scholarship disbursements, or register for health insurance without it.

What the Student MUST Know (Key Rules)
Tax Identifier
Issued Immediately

You receive a paper certificate (Certificato di Attribuzione) on the spot during your office visit.

Verify Spelling

Check that your name, date, and country of birth match your passport 100% to avoid administrative mismatches.

Lifetime Validity

Your fiscal code never expires and remains the same even if your permit or residency status changes.

Service Cost 100% Free of charge
Issuing Office Any local branch of Agenzia delle Entrate
Definition
What is it? (Che cos'è?)

The official polycarbonate plastic electronic ID card issued by the Italian Ministry of the Interior via your local municipality (Comune). It features a microchip with your photo, fingerprints, and digital identity credentials (CIE ID).

Legal Purpose
Why is it important? (Perché è fondamentale?)

It serves as your primary photo identification across Italy, allowing you to leave your bulky physical passport locked safely in your room. It is also required by many Italian banks to unlock full online banking.

What the Student MUST Know (Requirements & Usage)
Identity Card
Prerequisite

Requires prior registration of your civil residence (Iscrizione Anagrafica / Residenza) at the Comune.

Validity Scope

Marked "Non valida per l'espatrio" for non-Italian citizens (valid within Italy; travel abroad requires passport + permit).

Digital PIN & PUK

Keep the PIN and PUK letters delivered with the card to activate level 3 digital authentication on government apps.

Official Cost €22.20 standard issue fee
Issuing Office Ufficio Anagrafe (City Hall) via prenotazionicie.interno.gov.it
Definition
What is it? (Che cos'è?)

The plastic card granting you access to Italy's universal National Health Service (SSN). It assigns you a personal family doctor (Medico di Medicina Generale / Medico di Base) in your neighborhood for all primary healthcare needs.

Legal Purpose
Why is it important? (Perché è fondamentale?)

It gives you 100% free visits with your designated GP, subsidized or free prescription medications (ricetta rossa/dematerializzata), referrals to specialist doctors, diagnostic tests, and access to emergency hospital care (Pronto Soccorso).

What the Student MUST Know (Healthcare Rights)
Public Health
Free SSN Coverage

Beneficiaries of international protection under UNICORE receive free mandatory SSN registration (Iscrizione Obbligatoria Gratuita).

Family Doctor

Select a GP located close to your student dormitory or campus for walk-in medical consultations and referrals.

Guardia Medica

For urgent night or weekend medical assistance when the GP clinic is closed, call the local Guardia Medica (116 117).

Registration Fee Free for UNICORE international protection holders
Competent Office Local Distretto Sanitario ASL (Sportello Scelta e Revoca)
Definition
What is it? (Che cos'è?)

A personal financial account providing an Italian IBAN (starting with IT...) and an attached debit payment card (Mastercard/Visa) for cashless payments, mobile banking, and bank wire transfers (SEPA).

Legal Purpose
Why is it important? (Perché è fondamentale?)

Universities and regional scholarship boards (DSU/EDiSU) cannot deposit monthly living allowances, book grants, or refunds into foreign or third-party bank accounts. You must hold an account in your exact legal name.

What the Student MUST Know (Banking Rules)
Financial Account
Conto di Base Gratuito

Under MEF Decree 70/2018 & ABI agreement, refugees and low-income students are legally entitled to open a fee-free basic bank account.

Postepay Evolution

A fast, popular alternative issued by Poste Italiane equipped with an Italian IBAN and contactless card.

Required Documents

Passport, Codice Fiscale, Permesso postal receipt (Ricevuta), and official UNICORE award letter.

Account Fees Free for Conto di Base; ~€5–€15 activation for Postepay
Where to Open Major Banks (Intesa Sanpaolo, UniCredit) or Ufficio Postale
Definition
What is it? (Che cos'è?)

The unified digital credential (Sistema Pubblico di Identità Digitale) that allows citizens and residents to securely log into all Italian government, regional, and public university portals using a single username, password, and smartphone app (2FA).

Legal Purpose
Why is it important? (Perché è fondamentale?)

Traditional passwords for public services are phased out across Italy. You need SPID or CIE ID to access your INPS portal, check your tax file on Agenzia delle Entrate, download official civil status certificates, and apply for regional student benefits (DSU).

What the Student MUST Know (Digital Setup)
Digital Identity
PosteID Counter Activation

The easiest free way is activating PosteID at your local post office counter with Italian ID/Passport and Codice Fiscale.

Personal SIM Required

SPID must be linked to an Italian mobile number registered in your own name.

Government IO App

Download the official government IO App on your phone to receive official communications and digital documents.

Service Cost 100% Free with PosteID or Lepida
Registration Register online at posteid.poste.it & verify at post office
Definition
What is it? (Che cos'è?)

A physical adhesive tax stamp printed on security paper containing a unique serial number, barcode, and date/time of issuance, used in Italy to validate official administrative documents and petitions.

Legal Purpose
Why is it important? (Perché è fondamentale?)

Italian administrative law requires a €16.00 duty stamp on your residence permit application (Modulo 1 of the Kit Giallo), university graduation degree petitions, and official state certificate requests.

What the Student MUST Know (Purchase & Sticking)
Tax Stamp
Sold at Tabaccherie

Buy it in cash or card at any local shop displaying the white "T" sign (Tabaccheria).

Functions Like Cash

Has no name attached. Stick it securely on the designated top-right box of Modulo 1.

Serial Code for Digital Portals

When applying for digital certificates online, enter the 14-digit serial code printed on the stamp.

Exact Price Exactly €16.00
Where to Buy Any local Tobacco shop (Tabacchi / Ricevitoria)
Definition
What is it? (Che cos'è?)

In Italian civil law, Domicilio is the temporary place where you conduct your studies or daily life, whereas Residenza is your official habitual abode registered in the population registry (APR) of your municipality.

Legal Purpose
Why is it important? (Perché è fondamentale?)

Official residence (Residenza) is required to obtain the plastic electronic ID card (CIE), long-term healthcare assignments, local parking permits, and converting your study permit into a 12-month job search permit after graduation.

What the Student MUST Know (Municipal Check)
Civil Registry
Accertamento dei Vigili

Within 45 days, municipal police (Vigili Urbani) will visit your address to verify you physically live there. Put your surname on the intercom!

Housing Contract

You need a registered student housing contract, dormitory occupancy certificate, or a landlord consent declaration.

Service Cost 100% Free of charge
Application Office Ufficio Anagrafe of your local City Hall (Comune)
Legal Rights
Tax & Tuition Fee Exemptions (Esenzione Tasse)

Under Italian Legislative Decree 68/2012 and regional DSU regulations, UNICORE scholarship holders are legally entitled to total exemption from university tuition fees and regional taxes (€156) via ISEE Parificato calculation. Scholarship stipends are non-taxable maintenance grants.

Living Costs
Regional Cost-of-Living Tiers in Italy

Living expenses vary significantly by region. Northern hubs (Milan/Turin/Bologna) require ~€750–€950/month; Central hubs (Rome/Florence/Siena) require ~€650–€800/month; Southern hubs (Naples/Bari/Palermo) average ~€450–€600/month.

What the Student MUST Know (Budget & Welfare Relief)
Economic Welfare
ISEE Parificato Calculation

Request an ISEE Parificato per Studenti Stranieri at a tax assistance centre (CAF) upon arrival to secure zero university fees and maximum cafeteria discounts.

Mensa & Food Cards

Access university cafeterias (Mensa) for meals at subsidized student rates (€2.50–€3.50) or request local partner food gift cards (e.g. Centro Astalli / Caritas).

Assolavoro Educational Grants

Refugee students employed through or registered with employment agencies can claim book subsidies, study material grants, and nursery support.

First 30–45 Days Buffer

Initial stipend transfers require bank account and permit receipt activation. Coordinate with your local university desk for initial emergency welcome vouchers.

University Tax €0 (100% Legally Exempt with ISEE Parificato)
Support Offices CAF Tax Office • University DSU Welfare Desk
Late Arrival Protocol
Delayed Travel Documents & Mid-Semester Arrivals

If delays in acquiring Travel Documents (CTD) or Visas cause you to arrive after academic lectures have begun, your academic standing is protected. Contact your Degree Program Director immediately to assign a departmental tutor and request lecture video recordings.

Questura Backlogs
Managing 8–10 Month Questura Delays

In metropolitan areas (such as Veneto, Rome, or Milan), fingerprinting appointments may be scheduled 8–10 months out. Your postal receipt (Ricevuta Postale) guarantees 100% legal protection, allowing you to study, work part-time, and renew healthcare without penalty.

What the Student MUST Know (Emergency Actions)
Troubleshooting
Istanza di Sollecito (PEC)

If you have an urgent graduation or academic deadline, the University International Desk can submit a formal certified email (PEC) requesting Questura to expedite your card.

CIMEA & EQPR over DoV

You do NOT need to wait months for a consular Dichiarazione di Valore (DoV). CIMEA Statements of Comparability and Council of Europe EQPR passports are legally binding equivalents.

Post-Graduation Status

Upon graduation, convert your study permit into a 12-Month Job Search Permit (Permesso Ricerca Lavoro / Attesa Occupazione Art. 39-bis) or explore international protection.

Official Legal Shield Ricevuta Postale is 100% legally binding during backlogs

Vital Rules

Crucial Legal Traps & Mistakes to Avoid

Important precautions to safeguard your legal status and avoid bureaucratic delays in Italy.

Travel Rules with the Postal Receipt (Ricevuta)

While waiting for your electronic card, you can travel directly to your country of origin and return to Italy. However, you cannot transit through other Schengen countries (e.g. Germany, France, Switzerland). Flights must be non-stop direct or transit through non-Schengen nations.

Never Insert Original Documents in the Kit

Insert only clear A4 photocopies into the yellow envelope. Never mail original passports, academic degrees, or original scholarship letters. Keep all originals with you to present physically during your Questura fingerprinting appointment.

Keep Your Payment Receipts Safe (Cedolino)

The postal slip (Cedolino) with your User ID and Password is your official legal proof of lawful stay in Italy. Take a clear photo/scan immediately and store digital copies in your cloud drive.
