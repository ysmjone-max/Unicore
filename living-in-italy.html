Legal & Civil Infrastructure

The 6 Pillars of Italian Bureaucracy

Everything you need to know about official requirements, costs, office appointments, and rights.

1. Permesso di Soggiorno Studio

QUESTURA & POSTE ITALIANE

The primary legal residence title for non-EU students in Italy. Renewable annually based on academic credit acquisition (30–36 CFU).

  • Total Cost: €116.46 (€70.46 electronic card + €30 postal fee + €16 Marca da Bollo)
  • Work Right: Allows up to 20 hours/week (1,040 hours/year) of legal employment
  • Ricevuta Rights: The postal receipt acts as legal proof of regular stay while waiting for the card

2. Codice Fiscale (Tax Code)

AGENZIA DELLE ENTRATE

A 16-digit alphanumeric tax code required for rental contracts, university enrollment, bank accounts, and health insurance.

  • Cost: 100% Free of charge
  • Where: Any local Agenzia delle Entrate office (walk-in or online appointment)
  • Documents: Original passport, Type D visa, university enrollment letter

3. Italian Bank Account / Postepay

BANCA / POSTE ITALIANE

Mandatory for receiving your UNICORE monthly living stipend and paying university fees.

  • Options: Postepay Evolution (with Italian IBAN) or Conto di Base Gratuito
  • Documents: Codice Fiscale, Passport, Ricevuta Postale, UNICORE scholarship letter

4. Tessera Sanitaria & Medico di Base

ASL (LOCAL HEALTH SERVICE)

Grants access to Italy's National Health Service (SSN) and free general medical consultations.

  • General Practitioner (GP): Choose a family doctor close to your dormitory
  • Coverage: Free visits, medical prescriptions, specialist referrals, emergency care

5. Carta d'Identità Elettronica (CIE)

COMUNE / UFFICIO ANAGRAFE

The official electronic plastic national identity card issued after civil residence registration.

  • Where: City Hall registry office (Anagrafe)
  • Cost: ~€22.20; valid for 10 years across Italy and the Schengen area

6. SPID Digital Identity

PUBLIC DIGITAL IDENTITY

Single digital login credential used to access Italian public administration portals, INPS, and DSU student services.

  • Providers: PosteID (free via Post Office) or LepidaID
  • Requirements: Italian ID or Passport, Codice Fiscale, Italian phone number

Vital Rules

Crucial Legal Traps & Mistakes to Avoid

Important precautions to safeguard your legal status and avoid bureaucratic delays in Italy.

Travel Rules with the Postal Receipt (Ricevuta)

While waiting for your electronic card, you can travel directly to your country of origin and return to Italy. However, you cannot transit through other Schengen countries (e.g. Germany, France, Switzerland). Flights must be non-stop direct or transit through non-Schengen nations.

Never Insert Original Documents in the Kit

Insert only clear A4 photocopies into the yellow envelope. Never mail original passports, academic degrees, or original scholarship letters. Keep all originals with you to present physically during your Questura fingerprinting appointment.

Keep Your Payment Receipts Safe (Cedolino)

The postal slip (Cedolino) with your User ID and Password is your official legal proof of lawful stay in Italy. Take a clear photo/scan immediately and store digital copies in your cloud drive.
