Peer-to-Peer Support

UNICORE Buddy Mentorship Program

Designed by senior scholars to guide incoming refugee students through every step of academic, bureaucratic, and cultural integration across Italian university cities.

Scholars Supporting Scholars

Why the Buddy Program Matters

Transitioning from an asylum country to an Italian university involves overcoming unfamiliar bureaucratic hurdles, academic cultures, and daily life routines.

The UNICORE Buddy Program pairs each incoming scholar with a senior international or Italian peer mentor. Buddies provide individualized guidance from pre-departure planning and arrival accompaniment to oral exam preparation and graduation.

Connect with a Buddy View Settlement Guide
Buddy Mentorship Pillars
  • Pre-Departure Orientation: Video calls, packing tips, and airport arrival planning.
  • First-Week Settlement: In-person accompaniment for Kit Giallo, SIM card, and Codice Fiscale.
  • Academic Mentorship: Study schedules, exam registration (esse3), and oral exam tips.
  • Community Inclusion: Language exchange, campus clubs, and city exploration.
The Mentorship Journey

Three Phases of Peer Mentorship

A structured timeline ensuring continuous support before, during, and after arrival in Italy.

1
Phase 1: Pre-Arrival (August–September)

Direct Contact & Travel Preparation

Before departure, incoming scholars are introduced to their assigned buddy via WhatsApp and email. The buddy answers questions about weather, clothing, university housing, and essential luggage items.

  • Initial introductory video call & welcome package
  • Packing checklist & voltage adapter advice
  • Airport arrival route & public transit instructions
2
Phase 2: First 30 Days (September–October)

On-the-Ground Settlement & Bureaucracy

Upon arrival, the buddy meets the student, assists with settling into dormitory accommodation, and accompanies them through key bureaucratic offices.

  • Buying an Italian SIM card & opening a bank account
  • Submitting the Kit Giallo at Poste Italiane Sportello Amico
  • Campus tour: libraries, student canteen (Mensa), and department offices
3
Phase 3: Academic Year (November–July)

Academic Excellence & Social Inclusion

Ongoing peer support during coursework and exam sessions. Buddies help navigate Italian exam formats, professor consultations, and social life.

  • Exam booking procedures on online university portals
  • Study groups & oral exam mock practice
  • Italian conversational tandem & community gatherings
Nationwide Network

Regional UNICORE Buddy Chapters

Peer network coverage across Northern, Central, and Southern Italian universities.

Northern Italy Chapter

18+ UNIVERSITIES

Covering university cities in Lombardy, Emilia-Romagna, Piedmont, Veneto, Liguria, and Trentino.

Key Hubs: Bologna, Milano (Statale, Bocconi, Bicocca, PoliMi), Torino (UniTo, PoliTo), Verona, Padova, Venezia (Ca' Foscari, Iuav), Trento, Genova, Brescia, Bergamo, Pavia, Ferrara, Modena-Reggio Emilia, Parma.

Central Italy Chapter

12+ UNIVERSITIES

Covering university cities in Lazio, Tuscany, Umbria, Abruzzo, and Marche.

Key Hubs: Roma (Sapienza, Luiss, Tor Vergata, Roma Tre), Firenze (UniFI, EUI), Pisa, Siena (UniSi, UniStrasi), Perugia, L'Aquila, Tuscia (Viterbo), Camerino, Macerata, Urbino.

Southern & Islands Chapter

11+ UNIVERSITIES

Covering university cities in Campania, Apulia, Sicily, Sardinia, and Calabria.

Key Hubs: Napoli (Federico II, L'Orientale, Vanvitelli), Bari (UniBa, PoliBa), Foggia, Salento (Lecce), Messina, Palermo, Catania, Cagliari, Sassari, Reggio Calabria, Magna Graecia.
UNHCR Community Programs

Beyond Campus: Community Matching & Outreach Networks

Expand your support network through nationwide mentorship initiatives pairing students with local Italian community members and refugee leaders.

UNHCR Community Matching

13 ITALIAN CITIES • 1,500+ MATCHES

A structured 1-on-1 mentorship scheme connecting refugee scholars with local residents to practice fluent Italian, discover the city, and build genuine community ties.

  • Active in: Bari, Bergamo, Bologna, Milan, Naples, Padua, Palermo, Parma, Rome, Turin, Ravenna, Verona, Ivrea
  • Personalized pairings based on shared academic or cultural interests
  • Learn more at: unhcr.org/it

Community Outreach Volunteers (COV)

VOLONTARI NELLE COMUNITÀ (VOC)

A network of refugee community leaders across Italy collaborating with UNHCR and local municipalities to provide community guidance and protect rights.

  • Active in: Milan, Bologna, Rome, Naples, Turin
  • Community orientation, social mediation, and service referrals
  • Partnership with PartecipAzione community projects
