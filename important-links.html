Important Links & Support Resources

Verified links and resources for Italian language learning, free legal support, psychotherapy, asylum documentation, credential recognition, and career pathways in Italy.

Italian Language

CPIA — Public Adult Education Centers

Public state schools across every Italian province offering certified Italian language courses (A1, A2, B1, B2) and certification exams free of charge.

  • Free state-certified Italian A1–C2 courses
  • Recognized certification for residence permits
Ministry of Education CPIA Portal

Italian Language

University Language Centers (CLA)

Campus language centers (*Centri Linguistici di Ateneo*) present at all 40+ UNICORE universities, providing intensive academic Italian courses for enrolled scholars.

  • Free academic Italian for enrolled students
  • Free placement tests and university credits (CFU)
Associazione Centri Linguistici Universitari (AICLU)

Legal Support

Centro Astalli (Jesuit Refugee Service Italy)

Specialized legal clinic providing free consultation on residence permits, international protection rights, family reunification, and administrative procedures in Italy.

  • Free legal advice on *Permesso di Soggiorno*
  • Assistance with travel document (CTD) renewals
+39 06 69700306

Legal Helpline

ARCI National Helpline for Refugees

National free toll-free telephone service offering multilingual orientation, legal information, and referral to local services for asylum seekers and refugees in Italy.

  • Free Toll-Free Number from Italian SIMs: 800 905 570
  • Available in English, French, Arabic, Tigrinya, Somali, Farsi
Toll-Free: 800 905 570 / +39 342 0929112

Post-Graduation Law

12-Month Job Search Permit (Permesso Ricerca Lavoro)

Under art. 39-bis D.Lgs. 286/98, graduates of Italian Master's degrees can convert their student permit into a 12-month job search residence permit upon graduation.

  • 12 months of legal residence to search for work in Italy
  • Easily convertible into a Subordinate Work permit (*Lavoro Subordinato*)
integrazionemigranti.gov.it (Ministry of Labour)
View Official Ministry Guidelines

24/7 Emergency

112 — Single European Emergency Number

Universal emergency number across Italy and Europe for immediate police (*Carabinieri/Polizia*), fire department (*Vigili del Fuoco*), or medical response.

  • Free call 24/7 from any phone even without SIM/credit
  • Immediate geolocation and multilingual operators
Call: 112 (Toll-Free, 24/7)
Call 112 Immediately
24/7 Helpline

1522 — National Anti-Violence & Stalking Helpline

Public service promoted by the Department for Equal Opportunities, active 24/7 to support victims of gender-based violence, domestic abuse, or stalking.

  • Free, confidential call available in Italian, English, French, Arabic, Spanish
  • Free online chat available at 1522.eu
Call: 1522 (Toll-Free, 24/7)
