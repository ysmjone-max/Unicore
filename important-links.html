CPIA — Centri Adulti

Ministero dell'Istruzione
State School

State educational centers across all Italian provinces providing free certified Italian language courses (A1, A2, B1, B2) and legal A2 certification exams for residence permits.

  • 100% Free daytime & evening Italian courses
  • Official language certification recognized by Questura
  • Over 130 main territorial centers across Italy
Website: mim.gov.it/cpia
Access: Free enrollment at your municipal center
Find Local CPIA Center

CLA — Centri Linguistici d'Ateneo

Italian University Network
University

Every Italian university operates an in-house language center offering specialized intensive Italian language courses, conversational tandem exchanges, and academic writing support for international students.

  • Free Italian courses tailored to university coursework
  • Academic presentation & oral exam preparation
  • Language tandem peer learning programs
Where: Your university's CLA department website
Registration: Free with UNICORE student badge
Check Campus CLA Desk

Centro Astalli

Jesuit Refugee Service Italy
Legal Clinic

Leading Italian humanitarian organisation providing free legal counseling, assistance with residence permit appeals, international protection status, and social orientation for refugees in Italy.

  • Free legal orientation on Questura procedures
  • Support with international protection renewals & conversions
  • Cultural mediation in English, French, Arabic, Tigrinya
Headquarters: Via del Plebiscito 107, Roma
Phone: +39 06 6992 5099
Email: astalli@jrs.net
Visit Centro Astalli

ARCI — Numero Verde Rifugiati

National Asylum Helpline
Toll-Free Hotline

National toll-free telephone counseling service operating nationwide to provide immediate free legal answers, advice on administrative blockages, and referrals to local territorial desks.

  • Free toll-free helpline accessible from any Italian phone
  • Multilingual operators (Italian, English, French, Arabic, Somali)
  • Active Mon–Fri from 09:00 to 18:00
Toll-Free Number: 800 905 570
Mobile / WhatsApp: +39 340 778 8487
Email: numeroverderifugiati@arci.it
Visit ARCI Portal

JumaMap — Servizi Rifugiati

UNHCR • ARCI Italia
National Map

The official national interactive geolocation map developed by ARCI and UNHCR Italy, mapping thousands of verified local health centers, legal desks, language schools, and social canteens.

  • City-by-city interactive search for local assistance desks
  • Filter by legal aid, medical care, Italian classes, shelter
  • Available in 15+ languages on web and mobile
Portal: jumamap.it
Coverage: Nationwide across all Italian regions
Search Map for Your City

SAMIFO — Salute Migranti Forzati

ASL Roma 1 • Centro Astalli
Public Health Center

Specialized public healthcare center dedicated to psychological, psychiatric, and physical health for refugees, asylum seekers, and forced migrants, with professional cultural mediators.

  • Trauma-informed psychotherapy & psychiatric consultations
  • Multidisciplinary medical visits (internal medicine, gynecology)
  • Fully free within the National Health Service (SSN)
Location: Via Luzzatti 8, 00185 Roma
Phone: +39 06 7730 7403
Email: samifo@aslroma1.it
SAMIFO ASL Roma 1

Diaconia Valdese — Servizi Inclusione

Commissione Sinodale Diaconia
Community Support

Official humanitarian partner of the UNICORE corridors, providing psychological counseling desks, community integration initiatives, language workshops, and individual social accompaniment across Italian cities (Turin, Milan, Florence, Rome).

  • Community drop-in centers (*Community Center*)
  • Psychosocial support and cultural accompaniment
  • Active student network support
Portal: diaconiavaldese.org
Email: migranti@diaconiavaldese.org
Visit Diaconia Valdese

CIMEA — Credential Recognition

Italian ENIC-NARIC Centre
Official Recognition

The official Italian national body evaluating foreign academic credentials. Issues Statements of Comparability and Statements of Verification for international university admissions and academic equivalence.

  • Official Statement of Comparability (*Attestato di Comparabilità*)
  • Special free credential evaluation procedures for refugee students
  • Direct digital transmission to Italian university registrars
Portal: cimea.it/en
Refugee Service: cimea.it/rifugiati
Visit CIMEA Portal

Council of Europe — EQPR

European Qualifications Passport for Refugees
European Council

Specialized European framework designed to evaluate and certify academic qualifications for refugees and displaced individuals who lack complete physical documentation or original diplomas.

  • Structured academic interview assessment by international evaluators
  • Validated qualification passport recognized across 15+ European countries
  • 100% Free of charge for UNICORE applicants and scholars
Council Portal: coe.int/eqpr
Language: English, French, Arabic
Explore EQPR Program

UNHCR "Welcome" Network

Working for Refugee Integration
Employer Network

Network of over 500 certified Italian enterprises, multinationals, and research organizations awarded by UNHCR for actively hiring and integrating refugee talent and university graduates into professional roles.

  • Direct job postings and graduate internship pipelines
  • Mentorship with corporate HR leaders
  • Certified inclusive workplace environments
Portal: welcome.unhcr.it
Platform: Search 500+ certified Italian partner employers
Visit UNHCR Welcome Portal

AI Career Advisor • UNHCR Partner
Career AI Tool

Intelligent online career advisory platform offering automated Italian CV creation, video interview simulation, and tailored job vacancy matching customized for international scholars in Italy.

  • Automated CV and Cover Letter builder tailored for Italian employers
  • AI mock interview practice and personalized feedback
  • Direct job alerts filtered by discipline and Italian region

Access: Free registration for UNICORE scholars

ESN Italia — Erasmus Student Network

National Student Volunteer Network
Student Union

Largest student association across 50+ Italian university campuses, organizing city orientation, housing assistance, language tandem exchanges, trips, and cultural integration events.

  • Campus buddy pairing and housing support
  • Student discount card (ESNcard) for travel, hostels, and culture
  • Weekly social gatherings and city discovery tours

112 — Numero Unico Emergenza

European Single Emergency Number
24/7 Toll-Free

Single emergency hotline for urgent medical assistance (Ambulanza / 118), Police (Polizia di Stato / 113), Carabinieri (112), and Fire Department (Vigili del Fuoco / 115).

Emergency Dial: Call 112
Cost: 100% Free even without SIM credit
Languages: Italian, English, French, Spanish, German

1522 — Anti-Violenza & Stalking

Presidenza del Consiglio dei Ministri
24/7 Toll-Free

National public helpline dedicated to preventing gender-based violence, domestic abuse, and harassment. Completely confidential with multilingual operators.

Toll-Free Dial: Call 1522
Live Chat: 1522.eu (Confidential Chat)
Languages: Italian, English, French, Arabic, Spanish
