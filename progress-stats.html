Program Milestones

Milestone Evolution: Pilot (2019) to UNICORE 6.0 (2024)

Official trajectory and verifiable outcomes reported by UNHCR and participating Italian universities.

Edition & Year Universities Applications Scholarships Awarded Countries of Asylum Key Historical Highlights & Placements
UNICORE Pilot (2019) 2 (Bologna, LUISS) 43 6 Selected Ethiopia Founding pilot; 100% of scholars successfully graduated and obtained international protection.
UNICORE 2.0 (2020) 10 200 20 Selected Ethiopia 15 graduates; 6 employed in Italy, 2 pursuing PhDs, 6 relocated internationally (UK, Norway, Denmark).
UNICORE 3.0 (2021) 24 491 46 Selected Ethiopia 29 graduates; 11 employed in Italy (including Banca IFIS), 6 PhD & research contracts (CNR Pisa, TU Chemnitz).
UNICORE 4.0 (2022) 33 356 49 Selected 8 African Nations First major expansion to Cameroon, Malawi, Mozambique, Niger, Nigeria, South Africa, Zambia, Zimbabwe.
UNICORE 5.0 (2023) 34 557 51 Selected 7 African Nations Standardized CIMEA qualification verification & EQPR integration; Calzedonia & Permira mentorship.
UNICORE 6.0 (2024) 35 1,063 63 Selected 9 African Nations Miles4Migrants 60%+ flight savings; CampusX Milan free housing; record 31% female scholar participation.
