Historical Growth

Milestone Evolution: UNICORE 1.0 to 8.0

How UNICORE expanded from a 2019 pilot project in Ethiopia to 13 eligible countries across Africa and Asia.

Edition & Year Universities Scholarships Eligible Countries of Asylum Key Milestones
UNICORE 1.0 (2019) 2 6 Ethiopia Pilot program launched with University of Bologna and Luiss Rome.
UNICORE 2.0 (2020) 11 20 Ethiopia Expansion to 11 Italian universities despite COVID-19 pandemic.
UNICORE 3.0 (2021) 24 43 Ethiopia Caritas and Diaconia Valdese formalize nationwide reception protocol.
UNICORE 4.0 (2022) 32 51 Cameroon, Malawi, Mozambique, Niger, Nigeria, South Africa, Zambia, Zimbabwe First major geographical expansion beyond the Horn of Africa.
UNICORE 5.0 (2023) 35 60+ Kenya, Niger, Nigeria, South Africa, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe Introduction of the European Qualifications Passport for Refugees (EQPR).
UNICORE 6.0 (2024) 38 70+ 10 African Nations Over 200 cumulative scholars enrolled; strong alumni network formed.
UNICORE 7.0 (2025) 40 80+ 12 Nations (including India & Bangladesh) First expansion into Asian countries of asylum.
UNICORE 8.0 (2026 - Current) 40 80+ 13 Nations (Bangladesh, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Ethiopia, India, Kenya, Mozambique, Niger, Nigeria, South Africa, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe) 432 Master's degrees offered; enhanced post-graduation transition support.
