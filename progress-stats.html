Program Milestones

Milestone Evolution: Pilot to Edition 8.0

How UNICORE evolved from a 2-university pilot in 2019 to a global model covering 13 eligible asylum countries.

Edition & Year Universities Scholarships / Arrivals Countries of Asylum Key Historical Highlights
UNICORE Pilot (2019) 2 (Bologna, Luiss) 6 Arrivals Ethiopia Founding pilot; Bereket Gebremichael becomes first UNICORE graduate.
UNICORE 2.0 (2020) 11 20 Arrivals Ethiopia Inclusion of Eritrean, Congolese, and Sudanese refugees despite COVID-19.
UNICORE 3.0 (2021) 24 43 Places / 37 Arrivals Ethiopia Caritas Italiana & Diaconia Valdese formalize nationwide reception protocol.
UNICORE 4.0 (2022) 33 69 Places / 51 Arrivals Cameroon, Niger, Nigeria First major geographical expansion beyond the Horn of Africa.
UNICORE 5.0 (2023) 35 61 Places / 51 Arrivals 7 African Nations Integration of CIMEA Statements and EQPR qualification passports.
UNICORE 6.0 (2024) 39 67 Places / 65 Arrivals 10 African Nations Miles4Migrants flight partnership; over 250 cumulative scholars enrolled.
UNICORE 7.0 (2025) 33 71 Arrivals 6 Nations Milestone: Over 300 refugee students reached since inception.
UNICORE 8.0 (2026) 40–41 78–80 Places 13 Nations 432 Master's degrees; UNICORE Students Association & PhD research at UniBo.
