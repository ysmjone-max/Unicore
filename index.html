Higher Education Pathways for Refugee Scholars in Italy

University Corridors for Refugees (UNICORE) provides recognized refugees with safe, regular, and fully supported pathways to pursue Master's degree programs across 40+ Italian institutions.

What UNICORE Offers

An Italian multi-stakeholder academic partnership offering safe, regular corridors for refugee scholars.

  • 100% University tuition fee waivers and free enrollment
  • Study visa facilitation & international flight coordination
  • Accommodation, monthly stipends & buddy mentorship
