Yohannes Sisay Molla

Origin: Ethiopia • Italian Master's Graduate & Mentor

"When you arrive as a refugee scholar in Italy, navigating bureaucracy, exams, and housing can be challenging. Having experienced these hurdles first-hand, I established the UNICORE Buddy Program action plan to ensure incoming scholars are guided and supported by peer mentors on campus."
