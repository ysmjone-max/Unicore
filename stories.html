Video Story

Yohannes Sisay Molla

Origin: Ethiopia • Italian Master's Graduate & Buddy Leader

"When you arrive as a refugee scholar in Italy, navigating bureaucracy, exams, and housing can be challenging. Having experienced these hurdles first-hand, I authored the UNICORE Buddy Program complete implementation plan to ensure incoming scholars are guided and supported by peer mentors on campus."

Historic First Graduate
Luiss Rome (2019 Pilot)

Bereket Gebremichael Kidanemariam

Origin: Eritrea • Luiss Guido Carli Graduate

Arrived in Italy in 2019 as part of the founding 6-student pilot corridor between Ethiopia and Italy, and made history by becoming the very first UNICORE student to complete a Master's degree.

View Luiss Milestone Post
Florence Graduate
UniFI

Jules Bitwayiki Mende

Origin: DR Congo • University of Florence (UniFI)

"When I was living in the refugee camp, books were my only window to the outside world. Arriving through UNICORE allowed me to complete my Master's in Natural Resources Management for Tropical Rural Development at the University of Florence."

UniFoggia

Gbreel Telbo

Origin: Sudan • Asylum: Uganda • University of Foggia

Three years after fleeing the conflict in Sudan, Gbreel is pursuing his dream of clinical research at the University of Foggia, working in laboratories to develop medical solutions for conflict-affected populations.

Divine
