Firsthand Experiences

Scholars Who Changed Their Trajectory

Authentic quotes and narratives gathered from UNICORE cohorts studying across Italy.

Jules
DR Congo • Former refugee in Ethiopia

"Coming from the Democratic Republic of Congo and living in a refugee camp in Ethiopia, my future felt completely paused. UNICORE did not just open the doors of a university; it gave me back my voice and human dignity. My tutors and fellow Italian students became like my second family. Education is the bridge that turns refugee camp struggles into global empowerment."

University of Florence Master's in International Relations & Global Studies
Gbreel Telbo
Sudan • Former refugee in Uganda

"After armed conflict forced me to flee Sudan to Uganda, the dream of continuing biomedical science seemed out of reach. Being selected for UNICORE transformed that despair into determination. Today, in Foggia's laboratories, I am acquiring the molecular and clinical research skills needed to discover cures and save lives."

University of Foggia Master's in Clinical and Experimental Biology
Mohtas Anwar Modier
Sudan • Nuba Mountains

"Growing up in the Nuba Mountains where schooling was repeatedly halted by war, my father always told me: 'Education is the only key to unlock success.' UNICORE in Rome proved him right. We don't just study for academic degrees; we study to gain the competence needed to rebuild our societies."

Luiss Guido Carli University Rome Master's in Global Management and Politics
Philibert & Magda
Scholars residing in Zambia

"Tertiary education for displaced youth is usually an impenetrable barrier. UNICORE dismantled that barrier with safety, dignity, and a world-class academic opportunity. Arriving in Northern Italy, we were received with open arms by the local community."

Northern Italy Universities Engineering & Sustainable Technologies
Divine
Cameroon • UNICORE Scholar

"The transition was challenging at first, but having a designated student buddy helped me navigate everything from Italian supermarket shopping to lecture exam registrations. The support network never lets you walk alone."

University of Milan-Bicocca Master's in Economics & Data Science
Almaz
Eritrea • Former refugee in Ethiopia

"As a female refugee in STEM, opportunities were scarce. UNICORE broke that ceiling for me. Graduating with honors in Environmental Engineering opened international career avenues I could never have imagined in the camp."

Politecnico di Torino Master's in Environmental Engineering
