Authentic Voices

In Their Own Words

Read direct reflections from UNICORE students who transformed displacement into academic and scientific achievements.

Jules
Origin: DR Congo • Asylum: Ethiopia

"When I was living in the refugee camp, books were my only window to the outside world. Arriving in Florence through the UNICORE corridor allowed me to step directly into an international university classroom. My Italian student buddy helped me navigate the city and library on day one."

University of Florence (UniFI) M.Sc. in International Relations and European Studies
Gbreel Telbo
Origin: Sudan • Asylum: Uganda

"The laboratories here in Foggia gave me the cutting-edge tools I always dreamed of. Every hour I spend doing biomedical research is driven by a deep desire to develop medical solutions that can save lives in conflict-affected regions."

University of Foggia (UniFG) M.Sc. in Clinical and Experimental Biology
Mohtas Anwar Modier
Origin: Sudan • Asylum: Ethiopia

"Education is the only key that unlocks the door of impossibility. At Luiss in Rome, I learned governance strategies alongside peers from over 40 countries. Today, as a graduate, I feel empowered to advocate for sustainable development and refugee rights worldwide."

Luiss Guido Carli University (Rome) M.Sc. in Global Management and Politics
Philibert & Magda
Origin: DR Congo • Asylum: Zambia

"We applied for UNICORE together from Meheba settlement in Zambia. The scholarship took care of all our fees, dormitory accommodation, and health insurance. Having that peace of mind allowed us to focus completely on our engineering coursework."

University of Brescia / UniBS M.Sc. in Civil & Environmental Engineering
Divine
Origin: Rwanda • Asylum: Kenya (Kakuma)

"From teaching classes in Kakuma camp with chalkboards to running computer simulations in Milan, UNICORE proved that talent is universal, even if opportunity is not. I am forever grateful for the warm welcome from the Italian university community."

University of Milano-Bicocca M.Sc. in Data Science & Applied Informatics
Almaz
Origin: Eritrea • Asylum: Ethiopia (Mai Aini)

"My advice to all refugee women is to never give up on higher education. The application process was rigorous, but my professors in Turin encouraged me every step of the way. Today I am conducting research in renewable energy systems."

Politecnico di Torino (PoliTO) M.Sc. in Energy and Nuclear Engineering
UNICORE.
