Academic Research

Research Papers & Doctoral Dissertations

Peer-reviewed studies, ethnographic research, and international case studies analyzing the UNICORE model.

Doctoral Dissertation (2026) University of Bologna

"Iperinclusività accelerata: il processo di reinsediamento degli studenti UNICORE"

Doctoral thesis by Luca Vittori (University of Bologna, AMS Dottorato). An in-depth ethnographic analysis examining academic inclusion, complementary pathways, and university reception models in Italy.

Key Research Topics:
  • Global Citizenship Education & university sponsorship.
  • Peer relationships as foundations for inclusive citizenship.
Access Thesis on AMS UniBo
Peer-Reviewed Journal Taylor & Francis

Journal of Ethnic and Migration Studies: Humanitarian Corridors

Peer-reviewed scientific study examining how complementary university pathways and civil society sponsorships in Italy create viable alternatives to irregular migration.

Key Research Topics:
  • Civil society and multi-stakeholder governance models.
  • Comparative analysis of Italian complementary pathways.
Read Journal Paper (Taylor & Francis)
Global Compact UNHCR Good Practice

UNHCR Global Compact: UNICORE as an International Model

Official case study classifying UNICORE as an international Good Practice involving Government + UNHCR + Universities + Civil Society + Foundations.

Key Case Insights:
  • Model replicated in France (UNIV'R) and EU-Passworld.
  • Presented as Italian Best Practice at the Global Refugee Forum.
