Anti-Fraud & Authenticity Advisory

Participation in the UNICORE program is completely free of charge. All submitted educational and refugee identity credentials undergo thorough verification by expert committees. Submitting fraudulent diplomas or false certificates is a criminal offense, resulting in immediate disqualification and notification to legal authorities.
